Teacher Cheat Sheet

Director PIN: 666 (also accepts TEACHER). Use Director Mode and choose option 6 to open this page.

Game Flow

  1. The game timer starts at 45 minutes.
  2. Each team visits all five physical locations in its assigned route, including the Recess doors table-of-values question.
  3. After the route, every team completes the billion-column place-value chart as the shared final question and earns fragment #5, worth 9.
  4. The Recess-doors table-of-values station awards fragment #6, worth 7.
  5. Each team solves its own Evil Mr. Pierre challenge and earns a class-seal digit. Have them record and report it.
  6. Each team enters six-fragment vault code 538497, then reports its seal digit to you.
  7. After all five teams report, open Director Mode, choose option 5, enter each digit in its team field, and unite the class seal.
